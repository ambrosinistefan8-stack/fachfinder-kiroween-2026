The demo uses fictional inventory records and an original workshop image created with OpenAI ImageGen. The artwork was generated from an instruction describing an unbranded, front-facing workshop shelf with six blue, yellow and red bins. The bundled markers were then generated and placed on the image by `scripts/make-samples.mjs`.
